Smallmouth Bass were present in moderate abundance during 2025 sampling. Catch rates indicate a stable population.

Diet composition was dominated by crayfish, consistent with expected feeding behavior for the species. Limited reliance on pelagic prey suggests minimal dietary overlap with Walleye.

Length-frequency distribution and PSD metrics indicate balanced size structure, with representation across stock, quality, and preferred size classes. Relative weight values were high, reflecting good overall body condition and adequate forage availability.

Overall, the Smallmouth Bass population appears stable and in good condition under current reservoir conditions.
