Walleye exhibited fair overall body condition in 2025 (mean Wr = 86.2), with strong representation of sub-stock and stock-sized individuals. Larger fish remain present but at reduced levels relative to 2024.

Relative abundance increased to 43% of the total catch, with CPUE reaching 1.12 fish/hr — the highest recorded under standardized sampling. This increase may be partially influenced by reduced reservoir elevation and increased fish concentration.

Diet and condition data indicate ongoing forage limitation and density-dependent stress, particularly among juvenile and stock-sized fish. These factors appear to be constraining growth into preferred and memorable size classes.

Recent improvements in PSD metrics suggest some recovery in size structure; however, continued monitoring and adaptive management are necessary to maintain long-term population stability.
